Common Details 
 
The complete heater will be IECEx certified Ex e (Certificate IECEx LCI 06.0005X) in accordance with 
(IEC 60079-0: 2011 and IEC 60079 – 7: 2006) for use in a Zone 1, gas group IIA/B Gb, T3 area. 
 
The terminal box will be rated to IP66, be fitted with an anti-condensation heater, and have an 
undrilled removable gland plate for cable entry. 
 
The heater bundle is designed to suit an ambient temperature of 43ºC. However, the client should 
ensure that the terminal box is not subject to direct sunlight at ambient temperatures above 30ºC.  
An optional sunshade is offered to reduce terminal box heating through solar gain. 
 
The heater would have an over-temperature RTD installed at the tubesheet. 
The heater would have an RTD strapped to the elements to monitor element temperature.  
The RTDs would be terminated in a separate Ex e junction box located adjacent to the main terminal 
box. 
“Element over temperature protection is provided but this should not be the sole means to trip the 
heater in the event of a low flow situation occurring. We recommend that additional low flow 
protection is provided upstream of the heater to inhibit heater operation.” 
 
The elements would be supported in a stainless steel segmental baffled assembly 300mm pitch which 
would be tack welded to tie rods. 
 
The terminal box would be designed to accommodate up to 2 x 3 core 120mm² power cables. 
These are maximum acceptable sizes only, given for information. Actual cable requirements will vary 
depending on individual circumstances and should be verified by others.

The heater would have 3 thermocouples (1 HiHi per stage & 1 spare) strapped to the elements to 
monitor element temperature.

terminal box.

heater in the event of a low level situation occurring. We recommend that additional low level

welded to tie rods.

The heater will be energized from 0-100%, in response to a 4…….20 mA control signal supplied from 
the client’s DCS. 
 
The thyristor stack would comprise of an inverse – parallel connected pair of thyristors in two of the 
three phases. The thyristors would be fired at the zero voltage point by a single cycle type thyristor 
driver; the nominal harmonics generation across all values would not exceed 5% THDV. 
 
Protection of the thyristor would be by means of rapid acting semi-conductor protection fuses and 
voltage transient suppression circuits. 
 
The thyristors would have minimum peak inverse voltage rating of 2.5 times the maximum RMS line 
voltage and could work continuously at full current in the maximum ambient of 30C. 
 
The thyristor driver circuits would not be sensitive to, nor produce radio frequency interference. 
 
Suitable protection would be incorporated into the equipment to ensure that faults in the thyristor 
power or trigger circuits would not cause damage to the control equipment. 
 
All secondary control wiring would be carried out using black, flame retardant, low smoke, zero 
halogen cable to IEC 331/332 with a minimum C.S.A. of 1.5mm.
