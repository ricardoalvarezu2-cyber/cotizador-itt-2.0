Shipping Instructions
Purpose
The purpose of the present document is to define the requirements for shipping the Equipment and Materials to Argentina that will be incorporated in the Project. The Supplier shall be liable –without limitation- for any additional expenses due to Supplier non-compliance with this document.
Shipping Documents
General Requirements
Information of the equipment and material to be shipped shall coincide exactly with the information given in the shipping documentation, such as the gross and net weights, measurements and quantities, and if any discrepancies are noticed between the existing information in the documentation and what is actually shipped, the Supplier will be charged with all penalties, tributes and extra charges which may be applied by any intervening Authority in the country of origin or in the country of destination as well as all damages and prejudices suffered by the Purchaser.
Shipping Documents must be issued in Spanish.
The documents required by Argentinean Customs Authorities are:
Commercial Invoice
Containing the following information:
- Purchase Order Number
- Purchase Order item number(s) and quantity (do not include more than one PO item per line)
- Supplier’s Name and Address 
- Importer’s Name and Address
- Pre-Shipment Inspection Point
- Description of the goods per Purchase Order including Tag numbers if corresponds
- Commercial Brand and Model number/name of each item.
- Net unit prices for each item
- Total Invoice showing 100% value stating terms of delivery (FOB, CFR, etc.)
- Currency
- Country of origin for each item
- Invoice Number and Date
- Statement that goods are new
- Supplier’s Bank and Banking account number
- Tax ID of supplier
- Text stating the following (in Spanish).:
“Declaramos bajo juramento que los datos que contiene esta factura son el fiel reflejo de la verdad y que los precios indicados son los realmente pagados o a pagarse. Declaro en igual forma que no existen convenios que permitan alteraciones en estos precios” 
Consigned to: 
Packing List
 It shall contain the following information:
- Purchase Order number
- Quantity of packages
- Description of the content: each piece, part, component or unit packed in every package must be identified with a sticker/mark to determine to which item in the commercial invoice belongs to 
- Type of package (case, crate, pallets, etc.), quantity of pieces, volume, dimensions (L x W x H), net and gross weight of each package
- Dimensions (length, width and height) of each package. Total volume.
- Hazardous goods code (where applicable) with flash point in Celsius degrees
- Desiccant presence and type shall be indicated (if any) with its related check date
Consigned to: 
Shipping Document (B/L, AWB, etc)
Shipper: Supplier….......
Consigned to:
Notify:		
Bill of Lading must be issued in a liner form, showing “Clean on Board” – “Freight Pre-paid”.
Note: Freight Amount to be paid shall be included in each B/L or AWB and must be pre-paid.
Cargo Manifest and Final Stow Plan 
Seven (7) running days in advance to the vessel arrival, the shipping company shall submit a Cargo Manifest to the Argentinean Customs.
For all the Purchase Orders placed in condition of delivery CFR (Cost and Freight) and DAP (Delivery at Place) not including the Maritime Transport Insurance, the Supplier is responsible for the fulfillment of this presentation. Within seven running days after the departure of the vessel, the Supplier must send the Cargo Manifest and Final Stow Plan to:
Certificate of Origin
In case of being requested the Supplier shall issue the Certificate of Origin along with the Commercial Invoice stating, in addition to the origin details, the Supplier’s name and address without abbreviations and the Commercial Invoice number.
The Certificate of Origin shall be consigned to:  
Special Documents
The Supplier will provide the following documents if so is required:
Shippers Declaration for Dangerous Goods
Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) including, but not limited to shipping, storage and handling of oils, paint, desiccants or other related goods
Material Test Reports (MTR) or Material Certifications (MC)
Special requirements and Supplier recommendations for special storage, care in handling or other special warehouse requirements
Other documents which will be expressly required in each case.
Shipping Release Notice
The Supplier shall send the Goods once he receives the corresponding Shipping Release Notice.
