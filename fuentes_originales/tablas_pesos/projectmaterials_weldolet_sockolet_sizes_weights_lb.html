Weldolet & Sockolet: Sizes & Types

Author: Matteo Pusterla|26 min read

Quick Reference

MSS-SP 97 dimensions for branch fittings: Weldolet (size-on-size and reducing, Sch 40/XS/160/XXS), Sockolet (Class 3000 for Sch 80, Class 6000 for Sch 160), Elbolet (for 90° elbows). Key dimension: center-of-run to outlet face. Weights in pounds.
