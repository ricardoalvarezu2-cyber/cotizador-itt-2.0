45° 3R Elbow Dimensions

A 45° elbow on a three-diameter centerline radius. It combines the gradual half turn of a 45 with the wide sweep of a 3R bend, for lines where both the direction change and the erosion rate have to be kept gentle.

A specialty turn.

You reach for this when a 45° offset sits in abrasive or high-velocity service and a standard 1.5D bend would wear at the outside of the turn. Conveying lines, slurry headers, and sand-laden production piping are the usual homes for it.

Check the space.

The three-diameter radius makes this a long fitting for the turn it produces. Confirm the center-to-end from the table before committing the spool layout.

Reach for it when

  • ›Abrasive service that also needs a half turn rather than a square one.
  • ›Pneumatic conveying offsets where product degradation at bends matters.
  • ›High-velocity lines where every turn is engineered for minimum loss.

How To Read These Dimensions

Like its 90 degree counterpart, a 45 on a three-diameter radius sits outside both ASME B16.9 and ASME B16.28. It is a manufacturer-standard fitting with ASME B16.25 welding ends and the usual ASTM material grades, so the center-to-end A below is the figure to design around.

O.D.
Outside diameterOutside diameter at the welding end, matching the pipe it welds to.
I.D.
Inside diameterBore through the fitting, set by the wall thickness.
T
Wall thicknessNominal wall at the welding end, matched to the pipe schedule.
A
Center to endFrom the intersection of the centerlines to the welding face. The three-diameter radius makes this longer than a standard 45, so check it against the spool before committing.

Common Questions

What is a 45 degree 3R elbow?+

A 45° butt weld elbow whose centerline bend radius is three times the nominal pipe diameter, rather than the 1.5 times used on a standard long radius elbow. The wider sweep reduces turbulence and spreads erosion over more of the outside wall.

When is a 3R worth it over a standard 45?+

When the line is abrasive, high velocity, or carries solids, and wear at the bends is what drives your maintenance interval. In clean, moderate-velocity service the standard 1.5D fitting is the sensible choice.

Do you stock 45° 3R elbows?+

Availability depends on size, schedule, and material. Send us the size, schedule, and grade and we will confirm what is on the floor and what would be a mill order.
